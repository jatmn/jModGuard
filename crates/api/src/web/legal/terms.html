    • Using jModGuard

    Use jModGuard only in servers where you have permission to use it. Follow Discord’s rules and the rules of each server. Keep your account secure. Do not use the bot to harass people, bypass permissions, or disrupt the service.

    Running a server

    Server administrators choose the bot’s permissions, rules, and log channels. Review those settings and check important moderation decisions. Automated rules can miss violations or flag allowed messages. The bot and dashboard may be interrupted or change over time. You can stop using jModGuard in your server by removing the bot.

    Privacy and questions

    The Privacy Policy explains what jModGuard stores and how to request deletion. Use the operator’s contact details below for privacy questions.
